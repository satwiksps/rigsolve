# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in rigsolve a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to people affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Technical disagreement is expected. Challenge compatibility claims and evidence without attacking the person who supplied them.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, matrix facts, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Moderation decisions should include an explanation when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through [@satwiksps](https://github.com/satwiksps). Do not open a public issue. Reports will be handled as promptly and confidentially as circumstances allow.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use the following community-impact guidelines when determining consequences:

1. **Correction:** A private written warning and clarification of the violation.
2. **Warning:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior and a specified period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban:** A time-limited ban from project interaction or contribution.
4. **Permanent ban:** Permanent removal from project spaces for sustained violations, harassment, or aggression.
